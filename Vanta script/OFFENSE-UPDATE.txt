Vanta autofarm offense update

Execute PracticalComplete.luau or dist/PracticalComplete.luau; both contain the same updated build.

Changes
- Open shots are no longer blocked by a clear driving lane.
- Removed the fixed half-second opening wait and 1.2-second post-pressure lockout.
- Uses current defender distance and estimated closeout time, including ping, to select a shot.
- Requires feet to settle; no timeout forces a moving shot.
- Prioritizes a viable rim finish, then an open shot, then an attack or separation move.
- Keeps attacking after beating the defender; route scoring favors forward progress and avoids predicted collisions.
- Adds the recorded right-hand X stepback when facing the rim and the landing point is clear, in range and within bounds.
- Waits for the dribble action/displacement before reevaluating the shot; unknown left-hand stepbacks are not invented.
- Removes random crossover selection, guessed hand changes, and the fixed 1.5-second move cooldown.
- Prevents a held guard key or stale lob steering from overriding farm offense.
- Resets possession-specific plans, releases owned shot/dunk inputs on stop, and limits status-log spam.
